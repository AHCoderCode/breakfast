THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Everything you need to know before the first bite.

Is this for takeaway or delivery? +

This ordering experience is designed for takeaway collection. Choose a pickup date and time during checkout.

Can I customise my breakfast? +

Absolutely. Many menu items have size, topping, milk, spice and extra options. Open an item to see its available choices.

Can I pay online? +

Online payment is not enabled in this demo. Choose pay at pickup or cash on collection. No card details are collected.

Has my order been sent to the café? +

This is a frontend demonstration. Orders are saved only in this browser and are not transmitted to restaurant staff. A real order service must be connected before launch.

Where do I collect my order? +

The collection address is currently an editable placeholder. Please do not rely on it as a real business location.
